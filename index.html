A legacy rooted in a century of care

Founded in the forest-fringed town of Nilambur, Kerala, our vaidyasala has carried forward the knowledge of the Ashtanga Hridaya and classical Ayurvedic texts, passed from teacher to student for over a century.

Today, traditional diagnosis, herbal formulations and Panchakarma therapies are offered alongside modern patient care, with the same principle as always: treat the person, not just the symptom.
