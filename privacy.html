Online consultation privacy notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This notice explains information collected when you request an online consultation from Nilambur Vaidyasala. It is intended to be a plain-language notice for the service and should be reviewed by the clinic's legal adviser before launch.

What we collect

We collect your name, age, gender, phone number, email address, selected doctor and appointment time, health complaint, duration and severity, current medicines, allergies, prior conditions or surgeries, pregnancy information where applicable, department-specific answers, and any reports or images you choose to upload. We also keep consultation status, payment identifiers and limited technical rate-limit data.

Why we use it

The clinic uses this information to prepare for and provide your consultation, contact you about the booking, process the consultation fee, send the meeting link and reminders, maintain clinic records, and handle support, security and legal obligations. The clinic's copy of the confirmation email includes the questionnaire summary for the treating doctor and clinic administrator.

Who can see it

Authorised clinic staff and the doctor assigned to your consultation can see the consultation details. Our hosting and service providers process information to operate the website and database (Vercel and Supabase), process payment (Razorpay), deliver transactional emails (Resend), and provide video meetings (Jitsi Meet). Uploaded reports are stored in a private bucket and clinic staff receive short-lived access links. We do not place health answers in the payment request or patient confirmation email.

How long we keep it

Consultation records and uploaded reports are planned to be retained for three years after the final consultation, then deleted, unless a longer period is required for clinical recordkeeping, payment, safety, dispute or other legal obligations. Unpaid drafts are deleted after 90 days. Payment records may be retained separately where required. You may request deletion sooner, subject to any applicable recordkeeping duty. Please ask the clinic if you need the current retention schedule.

Your requests

You may ask to access, correct or request deletion of information by emailing niranjans@nilamburvaidyasala.com or calling 9446549329. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where a legal or clinical recordkeeping obligation applies. The clinic will respond through the same contact channel.

Telemedicine limitations

This service is not for emergencies. For chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms or loss of consciousness, go to the nearest hospital or call 112.

A video consultation does not include a physical examination. The doctor may decide that video care is unsuitable and advise an in-person visit or emergency care. Do not delay urgent treatment while waiting for an online appointment.

By booking, you are asked to consent to the collection and use of the information described above and to these telemedicine limitations.
